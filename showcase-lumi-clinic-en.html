LUMI Clinic

An editorial beauty clinic landing page that earns trust with a clear price list and an honest course timeline — no faked before/after photos.

View live site
✨ project overview

Editorial restraint, in a category full of exaggeration.

LUMI Clinic uses a porcelain-and-ink editorial palette and real photography instead of the retouched, oversaturated look common in beauty-clinic marketing. Pricing is presented as a clear list, and results are shown as an honest course timeline rather than manufactured before/after comparisons.

What stands out

  • Porcelain-and-ink editorial palette instead of typical beauty-clinic saturation
  • Transparent price-list layout

  • Real photography throughout
🎯 who this fits

Good fit for clinics that want to earn trust, not just attention.

This suits beauty and wellness clinics that want to differentiate on trust and transparency — clear pricing, honest expectations — rather than competing purely on before/after spectacle.
