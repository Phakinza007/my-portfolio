SolarPeak

A solar company landing page that borrows the visual language of an engineering spec sheet instead of a typical green-energy marketing page.

View live site
✨ project overview

A technical look for a technical purchase.

SolarPeak needed to sell a technical, high-consideration purchase — so instead of the usual green-gradient energy-brand template, the page borrows the language of an engineering spec sheet: real field photography, a comparison datasheet, and blueprint-style figures that signal competence before a single word of copy is read.

⚡ highlights

What stands out

  • Engineering-spec-sheet visual language instead of generic green energy branding
  • Real field photography, not stock imagery
  • Comparison datasheet for evaluating package options
  • Blueprint-style figures that signal technical competence
🎯 who this fits

Good fit for technical, high-consideration purchases.

This style works for solar, HVAC, and other technical home-improvement businesses where the buyer is comparing specs and wants to see competence, not just a friendly brand voice.
