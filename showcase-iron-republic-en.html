Project overview

A few minutes gets you the whole picture

Iron Republic needed a homepage that communicates energy immediately and pushes every visitor toward one clear action — signing up. The page uses a stark black-and-red palette, a bold type-led hero, and a stats strip that sells results instead of amenities.
