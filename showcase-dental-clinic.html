BRIGHT Dental Clinic

A dental clinic landing page in a clean teal medical palette, built around transparent pricing and a working booking form.

View live site
✨ project overview

A medical look that reduces anxiety instead of adding to it.

BRIGHT Dental Clinic uses a clean, teal-forward palette to feel clinical without feeling cold. The page leads with a transparent price table so visitors know what to expect before they book, introduces the dentists by name and photo, and closes the loop with an online booking form instead of a phone-only call to action.

⚡ highlights

What stands out

  • Transparent price table shown up front
  • Dentist profiles with names and photos, not an anonymous “our team”
  • Clean teal medical palette that reads calm, not clinical
  • Working online booking form
🎯 who this fits

Good fit for healthcare practices where trust and price clarity drive bookings.

This works for dental, medical, and healthcare practices where patient anxiety and price uncertainty are the two biggest things stopping someone from booking — both addressed directly on the page.
