MuseRoom is a small gallery for light-based work — three rooms on one floor, each built around a single material: projection, filament, and the sodium glow of the corridor that links them. Sessions run morning, afternoon and dusk.

Three shows.
One clean route.

The floor reads as one walk: you enter through the red light of Room 01, cross the dark of Room 02, and leave along the corridor that is itself the third work. Allow forty minutes for all three.

Soft Signal

A single backlit panel, held at the brightness of a screen left on overnight. The vault is otherwise unlit, so the colour lands on the stone before it lands on you.

Afterimage Notes

Twelve metres of lit filament hung in a blacked-out room. Stand still for a minute, then close your eyes — the drawing stays with you longer than it stays on the wire.
