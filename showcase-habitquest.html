HabitQuest

A full-stack habit tracker that turns daily habits into an RPG adventure — quests, monsters, and a hero that levels up with you.

View live site Read the full case study
✨ project overview

Habit tracking with actual stakes.

HabitQuest reframes habit tracking as an RPG: complete real-life habits to finish quests, defeat monsters, and level up a hero, instead of just checking boxes on a streak counter. It's a full-stack build with real user accounts and persistence, not a static demo.

Gamification Habit Tracker
⚡ highlights

What stands out

  • Real-life habits mapped to RPG quests and monster battles
  • Hero leveling system tied to consistency, not just streaks
  • Full-stack build with real accounts and persistence
  • Playable live, not a mockup
🎯 who this fits

Good fit for showing product thinking, not just UI.

This project is less about a client vertical and more about demonstrating product thinking — taking a plain utility (habit tracking) and finding a mechanic that makes people actually want to open the app again.
