RATRI Restaurant

A fine-dining landing page built around one real job: getting a table reserved, not just admired.

View live site
✨ project overview

An elegant page built around a working reservation flow.

RATRI is a concept for a modern Thai fine-dining restaurant where the visual language needed to feel as considered as the menu — dark, elegant, restrained. The centerpiece is a validated reservation flow, not just a phone number, so the page does the job a restaurant site is actually for.

What stands out

  • Elegant dark palette that matches a fine-dining brand
  • Reservation flow with real front-end validation

  • Thai-first copy and layout for a local dining audience

Good fit for hospitality brands where booking is the real goal.
