Why Solar

Built for the Long
Run

Cut Your Electricity Bill by 40% on Average

Generate your own power in the hours you use the most of it. Anything you don't use is exported to MEA or PEA under the residential rooftop scheme, earning feed-in credits against next month's bill.

Zero-Emission Clean Energy

The average SolarPeak rooftop is 7.5 kW and offsets about 5.2 tonnes of CO₂ a year — roughly what 250 newly planted trees absorb annually.

Energy Independence

With optional battery storage, keep the essentials running through an outage and insulate yourself from the Ft tariff adjustments that move your bill every four months.

Increase Property Value

A paid-off array is a fixed running cost a buyer inherits. Homes with solar consistently sell faster, and for up to 4% more, than comparable properties without it.
