Elasticshop Gaming Top-Up

A dark, yellow-accented design system for an automated game top-up platform, covering four full screen flows from product selection to confirmation.

View live site
✨ project overview

A full design system, not just a landing page.

Elasticshop needed more than one hero screen — it needed a coherent system across four flows: browsing games, selecting a top-up package, checkout, and confirmation. The dark, high-contrast yellow palette borrows gaming UI conventions so it feels native to its audience rather than generic e-commerce.

Design System Gaming UI Figma Full Stack
⚡ highlights

What stands out

  • Four complete screen flows designed as one system
  • Gaming-native dark UI with high-contrast yellow accents
  • Built first in Figma, then implemented full-stack
  • Consistent component system across every screen
🎯 who this fits

Good fit for niche platforms with their own visual language.

This approach works for gaming, esports, and other niche digital platforms where a generic SaaS look would feel out of place, and where the product genuinely needs more than one screen to design properly.
