Iron Republic

A gym brand homepage built around one job: get a visitor to sign up before they scroll past the hero.

View live site
✨ project overview

High-contrast branding built for a single conversion action.

Iron Republic needed a homepage that communicates energy immediately and pushes every visitor toward one clear action — signing up. The page uses a stark black-and-red palette, a bold type-led hero, and a stats strip that sells results instead of amenities.

Fitness Brand
⚡ highlights

What stands out

  • Stark black-and-red palette built for high visual energy
  • Type-led hero with a single, unambiguous primary CTA
  • Stats strip that leads with results, not amenities
  • Mobile-first CTA stacking so sign-up stays one tap away
🎯 who this fits

Good fit for brands that sell intensity, not comfort.

This style works for gyms, combat sports studios, and fitness brands whose whole pitch is energy and results — where a soft, pastel layout would undersell the brand instead of representing it.
